Privacy Policy

Xiaomi Scanner rclone is a personal, single-user OAuth client used solely by its owner to synchronize the owner's Xiaomi Scanner project files with the owner's Google Drive through rclone.

The application is not offered to the public.

No personal data is collected, sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties by this application.

Google Drive access is used only to transfer files between the owner's own device and the owner's own Google Drive account.

No Google user data is retained by any external service operated for this application.

Questions about this application may be directed to its owner through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
